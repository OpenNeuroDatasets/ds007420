# Multi-Distance fNIRS Dataset Generated During Ball-Squeezing and Motion-Artifact Induction Tasks

Multi-distance fNIRS recordings from 12 healthy adults performing a resting-state task, ball-squeezing tasks, and a motion-artifact induction task.

## Authors

Shibo Zhou, De'Ja Rogers, David A. Boas, Meryem A. Yücel, Yuanyuan Gao

## Citation

Zhou, S., Rogers, D., Boas, D. A., Yücel, M. A., & Gao, Y. Multi-Distance fNIRS Dataset Generated During Ball-Squeezing and Motion-Artifact Induction Tasks. OpenNeuro. [Dataset] doi:10.18112/openneuro.ds007420.v1.0.4

## License

CC0

## Participants

12 healthy young adults (6 female, 6 male; 23.2 ± 5.2 years; range 18–37 years).
